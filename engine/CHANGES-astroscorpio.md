# Changes made for astroscorpio.com

The engine is the jyotisha package as of 14 Oct 2026, with the changes below. The first group keeps the engine's existing results. The corrections from the `yogas.py` rule rows on change results on purpose, to match the cited texts.

| File | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `ephemeris.py` | `moon_rise_set(start, end, lat, lon)`, new | Panchang moonrise and moonset. Disc centre with refraction, the same convention as `sunrise`. |
| `ephemeris.py` | `luminaries(jd, ayanamsa)`, new | Sun and Moon only, computed exactly as `positions()` computes them. |
| `ephemeris.py` | `crossings(body, start, end, width, ...)`, new | Sign (width 30) and nakshatra (width 360/27) ingresses, with the boundary longitude and the speed there. `ingress_dates` is unchanged. |
| `muhurta.py` | `tithi_at`, `nakshatra_at`, `yoga_at`, `karana_at`, `_elongation` and the Moon-sign scan in `day()` use `luminaries()` | Identical values, about 5x faster panchang (404 ms to 70 ms per day). Verified byte-identical against the pre-change output. |
| `muhurta.py` | `evaluate`, `check` and `find` accept `natal_moon=None` / `natal_lagna=None` | Muhurta without a birth chart. Rules relative to the birth chart (tara, chandrashtama, lagna 8th from birth, Moon 4th/12th from the birth Moon) are skipped rather than guessed. |
| `ashtakavarga.py` | `_bhinna_cached` stores a weakref and checks identity; the cache is bounded | A bug in long-running processes. The cache was keyed on `id(chart)`, and CPython reuses a freed object's id, so a new chart could be served a dead chart's bindus. |
| `yogas.py` | Sankhya yoga reason reads "1 sign" / "5 signs" instead of "5 sign(s)" | Display text. |
| `yogas.py` | Every yoga carries `source` (text, verse, edition, printed page in `SOURCES`), `reckoned_from`, `grahas`, `notes`, `variants`, `checks` and `reads_lagna`; `all_yogas(chart, lagna_known)` | Each claim can be traced to a page. Yogas with no classical source found (Budha-Aditya, Chandra-Mangala, Kala Sarpa) have `source=None`. Without a birth time only yogas that hold on sign positions are returned. |
| `yogas.py` | Rules corrected to the texts: Sunapha/Anapha/Durudhara leave out the Sun (Phaladipika 6.5, Brihat Jataka 13.3); Kemadruma reports PD 6.5's minority cancellations as checks; Gaja Kesari per PD 6.14 with BPHS's stricter rule as a variant; Shakata cancelled with the Moon in a kendra from the lagna (PD 6.14); Adhi needs Mercury, Jupiter and Venus all in the 6th-8th, from the Moon (BJ 13.2) or the lagna (PD 6.42); Amala counts Mercury (PD 6.19); Saraswati needs Jupiter dignified and strong (PD 6.26); Lakshmi per BPHS with PD 6.24 as a variant; Mahapurusha own sign or exaltation from the lagna and the Moon (PD 6.1, 6.4); Raja yoga adds BPHS's one-way link and v.15 (houses 3, 6, 11); Neecha Bhanga adds PD 7.27; Nabhasa yogas need exactly the named houses, Srak/Sarpa per BJ 12.2's note, Gada, Vajra, Yava and Ardhachandra added, ashraya yields to akriti (12.12), sankhya only alone (12.10); Dhana yogas are BPHS 42's fourteen combinations | Checked against public-domain printings (Phaladipika 1937, Brihat Jataka 1912, BPHS 1939). "Strong" as shadbala at or above BPHS's required total is our reading. |
| `chart.py` | The Moon exalted only to 3° Taurus and Mercury to 15° Virgo, mulatrikona from 3° and 15° (BPHS 3.52-53); Rahu and Ketu dignities from BPHS Dasa-phala 35-36, else `None`; benefic Moon in tithis 9-22 (BJ 2.5, 1912 note); Mercury malefic only joined with a malefic (BPHS 3.11) | As printed in those texts. |
| `strength.py` | Saptavargaja values 45/30/20/15/10/4/2 (BPHS 28.3), mulatrikona spans applied by degree; paksha bala per BPHS 28.10-11 (the Moon and Mercury in the benefic group, no doubling); the Sun's required total 6.5 rupas (28.32-33) | As printed. Applying the mulatrikona spans inside the vargas is our reading. |
| `panchanga.py`, `muhurta.py` | `vara_index` names the weekday by the local civil date of the sunrise (`tz`), or local mean time when no zone is given; `compute`, `muhurta.day`, `check` and `find` pass the zone | The UT date put every sunrise east of about UTC+6 (Sydney, Tokyo, Singapore, Auckland) on the previous weekday, which also shifted Rahu kalam, horas and choghadiya there. |
| `rare.py` | `UTPATA`, `KANA`, `SIDDHI` (the other columns of MC 1.30) and `RAVI_YOGA_COUNTS` (MC 1.27), new | The panchang names these weekday-nakshatra yogas. Monday's Kāṇa is Abhijit, the 28th nakshatra, which the 27-nakshatra tables leave out. |
| `hypotheses.py` | `VEDHA["Venus"]`: 11 is blocked from the 3rd and 12 from the 6th (the two were swapped) | Phaladipika 26.8 pairs the good houses 1-5, 8, 9, 12, 11 with the vedha houses 8, 7, 1, 10, 9, 5, 11, 6, 3; Muhurta Chintamani 4.3 agrees. |
| `muhurta.py` | `BHADRA_FACE_TAIL` and `bhadra_face_tail(tithi, start, end)`, new | Bhadra's face and tail by tithi (MC 1.44), with the 5 and 3 ghatis fixed (Dharmasindhu pp. 94-95). |
| `ephemeris.py` | `declination_view(jd, "Sun"|"Moon")`, new | Tropical longitude, declination with latitude, daily motion and semi-diameter, for mahapata. |
| `muhurta.py` | `mahapatas(t0, t1)`, new | Mahapata as equal declinations of the Sun and Moon (MC 1.34 commentary), found per Surya-siddhanta 11 (Burgess), with its half-duration from 11.14-15. |
| `rare.py` | `KANA[1] = "Abhijit"`, `ABHIJIT`, `nakshatra_28()`, new | MC 1.30 counts 28 nakshatras; Abhijit is the last quarter of U.Ashadha and the first fifteenth of Shravana (Muhurta-martanda 1909, p. 139). |
| `muhurta.py` | `Day.bhadra_parts` and `Day.mahapatas`; `evaluate` vetoes Bhadra's face, lifts the Bhadra veto in its tail and under MC 1.44's day/night rule, vetoes utpata, kana (MC 1.30, over 28 nakshatras) and mahapata, and supports siddhi | The lead's rulings of 15 Oct 2026 (utpata, kana and mahapata take marana's veto; Ravi yoga stays display-only). |
| `muhurta.py` | `kalamsa(name, sr, ss, lat, lon)`, `jupiter_venus_states(days, lat, lon, counts=None, venus_direct=10.0)`, `planet_states_around(start, end, lat, lon)` and `OLD_INFANT_DAYS`, new | Jupiter and Venus set by Surya-siddhanta 9.6-7's time-degrees (Jupiter 11; Venus 8 retrograde, 10 direct), measured from the difference in rising or setting times at the place, with Muhurta Chintamani 5.27's days of infancy and old age by the side of the sky. Used by the marriage and house-entry lists and the "home" act. |
| `muhurta.py` | The "home" profile follows Muhurta Chintamani ch. 13 (`_home_vetoes`); `evaluate`, `check` and `find` take `door` and `old_house` | The lead's decisions of 15 Oct 2026 on the house-entry sources. Vetoes: the month (13.1, 13.4; Guru's bar on the Sun in Cancer, Virgo or Aquarius), an adhika month, Jupiter and Venus set, infant or old and Jupiter in Leo or Capricorn (1.46-48, 5.27; not for an old house, 13.2), Sunday and Tuesday, a movable lagna or navamsa, any planet in the 4th or 8th, the Moon in the 1st, 6th or 12th (13.3-4), the pot chakra (13.6), and with a door direction its tithi class and the Sun on the left (13.5). The tithi set is 1-15 less the rikta tithis and Amavasya; Kartika and Margashirsha are a middling defect. The old profile favoured a fixed tithi set, only rewarded fixed lagnas, penalised only malefics in the 4th and had no month, Jupiter/Venus or pot-chakra rule. |
| `muhurta.py` | The "home" act vetoes Sunday and Tuesday (`HOME_BARRED_VARAS`) | Muhurta Chintamani 13.4 lists them with the rikta tithis, the movable lagna, Amavasya and Caitra, which are vetoes; the lead confirmed it on 15 Oct 2026. |
| `muhurta.py` | A "mundan" act (`Profile.mundan`, `MUNDAN_STARS`, `_mundan_vetoes`, `_mundan_child`); `evaluate`, `check` and `find` take `natal_month` and `eldest` | The first tonsure per Muhurta Chintamani 5.26-30 and Dharmasindhu p. 137, as the lead approved on 15 Oct 2026: the month, the Sun's course, Jupiter and Venus, the twelve stars, tithis and weekdays, a benefic's lagna, the 8th and the kendras as vetoes, and with a chart the birth star, birth month and 8th sign from birth. Other acts are unchanged. |
